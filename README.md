# Tayseer Digital Adoption – Capstone Project

SDAIA Academy · SDA-DSC-112 Data Visualization & Storytelling


## What we did
We analysed the Tayseer services dataset to decide where to direct SAR 40M to help lagging regions reach the 65% digital-adoption target. We then built a seven-slide executive presentation around that decision.

Key findings (December 2025):
- National digital adoption is 66.2%, above the 65% target.
- 8 of 13 regions are below 65%.
- Najran, Northern Borders, Al-Baha and Jazan are furthest behind, at 2–4 points below target.

Recommendation: allocate the SAR 40M to Najran (15M), Northern Borders (9M), Al-Baha (8M) and Jazan (8M), and review progress after 90 days.

## Dashboard
Built in Power BI. It contains:
- KPI cards: national adoption % and the number of regions below target
- Line chart: national adoption trend from 2022 to 2025, with a 65% target line
- Bar chart: adoption by region, sorted lowest first, with below-target regions highlighted and a 65% reference line
- Region filter

Digital Adoption % = Σ(digital adoption % × unique users) ÷ Σ unique users
